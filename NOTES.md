# NOTES — The One-Line Exit (u-kreutzx-the-one-line-exit-1dc652d1)

## Chosen line (101 chars, single line)

> I hereby resign — my two weeks' notice is the longest project this company has ever finished on time.

## Why this one

- Reads like a real, sendable resignation letter ("I hereby resign").
- Surprise punchline: the twist lands on the last three words — the
  notice period itself is the company's only project ever finished on
  time, which reframes quitting as the one on-time delivery.
- Specific voice: an employee who has lived through the company's
  chronically late projects, not a generic complaint.
- Quotable and forwardable: one clean sentence, no list of grievances.

## Candidates considered

1. "Dear Manager, I quit: the exit interview is the first meeting this
   company has ever scheduled that I actually want to attend." (125)
   — good, but the punchline is a little wordy.
2. "Effective today, I resign — I finally got the raise I asked for,
   from the company across the street." (100) — solid twist, but the
   raise-across-the-street joke is a familiar trope.
3. "I quit: the only thing this job ever promoted was my anxiety, and
   it didn't even need an interview." (99) — funny, but leans on the
   generic "my job gave me anxiety" complaint the brief warns against.
